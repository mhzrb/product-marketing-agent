# Kobalt IT Supply - Brand Voice

Kobalt IT Supply is a fictional B2B IT retailer used for this demo. Our customers are IT managers,
office managers and procurement teams at small and mid-sized businesses (MKB) in the Netherlands
and Belgium. They buy laptops, monitors, networking gear, storage and peripherals in volume.

## Voice principles

- **Practical, not hyped.** We talk like a knowledgeable colleague from the IT department. We state
  what a product does, who it suits and what it costs. We do not shout.
- **Specific beats superlative.** Prefer "16 GB RAM and a 512 GB SSD" over "powerful performance".
  A concrete spec from the data sheet is always better than an adjective.
- **Respect the reader's time.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Lead with the benefit that
  matters to a business buyer: reliability, manageability, total cost of ownership, rollout effort.
- **Confident, never absolute.** We say "designed for", "suited to", "helps teams". We never promise
  outcomes we cannot prove ("eliminates downtime", "guaranteed results").
- **Honest about gaps.** If a spec is not in the data, we leave it out. We never guess a number.

## Tone by channel

- Product description: informative, calm, third person ("The ... is built for ...").
- Ad copy: one clear benefit plus one concrete fact. No exclamation marks.
- Social post: friendly and human, at most one emoji, no more than two hashtags.
- Email subject lines: plain and specific. No ALL CAPS, no clickbait, no fake urgency.

## Words we like

reliable, manageable, ready for rollout, built for teams, compatible with, designed for, suited to,
straightforward, dependable, clear pricing.

## Words we avoid

revolutionary, game-changing, ultimate, unbeatable, perfect, magic, best-in-class (unless a
third-party ranking is provided in the product data), cutting-edge used as filler.
