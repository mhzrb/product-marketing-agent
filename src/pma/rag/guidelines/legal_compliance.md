# Legal and Compliance Notes for Marketing Copy

This is an internal summary for writers. It is not legal advice; legal review remains with the
compliance team.

## Pricing

- Business-to-business prices are quoted excluding VAT. Write "excl. VAT" (Dutch: "excl. btw").
- Show the price exactly as supplied. Do not round, and do not add "from" or "starting at" unless
  the data does.
- Do not mention discounts, promotions or "free shipping" unless the product data states them.

## Comparative advertising

- Comparisons must be objective and verifiable, and must not mislead or denigrate. Without
  evidence supplied in the product data, do not compare with competitors or with earlier
  generations.

## Email marketing

- Subject lines must not mislead about the content of the email.
- Promotional email requires consent or an existing customer relationship (GDPR / Telecommunications
  Act). Subject lines never imply a personal relationship that does not exist ("Re:", "Fwd:").

## Warranty and returns

- Quote only the warranty period given in the data. Do not extend, summarise as "lifetime" or
  call it "no-risk".

## Environmental and sustainability statements

- Green claims need a named certification in the product data (for example an energy label). Without
  it, omit sustainability language entirely.

## Personal data and trust

- Marketing copy never includes customer names, testimonials or review quotes unless they are part
  of the supplied product data.
