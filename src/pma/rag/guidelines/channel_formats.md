# Channel Formats and Length Limits

All lengths are in characters, including spaces. The automated evaluator enforces these limits.

## Product description

- Length: 60 to 600 characters.
- Structure: what it is and who it is for, then two or three concrete specs, then the price
  (excl. VAT) if known.
- Mention the product name once. Plain prose, no bullet lists, no emoji.

## Short ad copy

- Length: 20 to 150 characters.
- One benefit, one concrete fact, product name included. No exclamation marks.
- Used for search ads and banner text, so it must make sense without surrounding context.

## Social post

- Length: 30 to 280 characters.
- Friendly tone. At most one emoji and at most two hashtags (for example #ITProcurement).
- One idea per post. Never invent offers, discounts or dates.

## Email subject lines

- Exactly three alternatives per language.
- Length: 10 to 60 characters each, all different from each other.
- Sentence case, no ALL CAPS words, no spam triggers ("FREE", "!!!", "act now").
- Prefer a concrete product or benefit hook: "New in stock: <product>" or "<Product> for hybrid
  offices".

## Bilingual output

- English and Dutch versions are written independently for the same facts. They do not need to be
  literal translations, but they must state the same facts and the same numbers.
- Language must match the label: no English sentences inside the Dutch text or vice versa, except for
  product names and established technical terms.
